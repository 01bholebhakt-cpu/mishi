Good to know

Delivery & policies

How to order

Add your pieces to the bag, enter your details and choose a delivery method. When you place your order, it reaches us as a ready message on WhatsApp. We confirm availability, share your delivery charges and payment details, then dispatch your parcel.

Delivery

We ship worldwide from Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Estimated delivery times are shown at checkout. Final delivery charges depend on your location and are confirmed with you before you pay.

  • India, standard and express
  • International
  • Gift packing available on request at checkout

Payment

Payment is made in advance, after we confirm your order on WhatsApp. We do not offer cash on delivery.

Returns & exchanges

All sales are final. We do not accept returns or exchanges, so please check the size guide on each product page and message us if you would like help choosing a size.

Questions

Can I see a piece before ordering?

Yes. Visit our store in Indore between 12 PM and 9 PM, or ask us for more photos on WhatsApp.

How do I choose my size?

Use the size guide on any product page. If you are between sizes, message us your measurements and we will advise.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, we ship worldwide. Choose an international address at checkout and we will confirm charges and timing with you.
